About the Event

The Sarawak Neurosurgery Update 2026 is the flagship neurosurgical workshop and conference organised by the Department of Neurosurgery, Sarawak General Hospital, through the Persatuan Jabatan Neurosurgery Sarawak (PJNS).

This three-day event brings together leading neurosurgeons, medical professionals, and nursing staff for an intensive programme of advanced workshops, expert lectures, and knowledge exchange covering the latest developments in neurosurgery.

Topics Covered

Neuro-Trauma Neuro-Vascular Neuro-Oncology Neuro-Intensive Care Paediatric Neurosurgery Functional Neurosurgery Neuro-Rehabilitation
